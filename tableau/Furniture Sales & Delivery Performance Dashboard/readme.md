# 📊 Sales & Delivery Performance Dashboard 

This folder contains a completed **Tableau dashboard** focused on **sales analysis and delivery performance evaluation**.  
The dashboard is designed to provide **clear visibility of key business KPIs**, sales trends, regional performance, and logistics efficiency.

---

## 🔹 Sales & Delivery Performance Metrics

**Goal:**  
Provide a **comprehensive overview of sales performance and delivery efficiency** by visualizing revenue, orders, product demand, regional distribution, and delivery timelines.  
The aim is to support **data-driven business decisions** related to sales growth, regional optimization, and logistics improvement.

**Technics Used:**  
- Tableau interactive dashboards.  
- KPI scorecards for monitoring.  
- Calculated fields and aggregations.  
- Time-series analysis for sales trends.  
- Geographic analysis with correctly mapped regions.  
- Visualizations: KPI cards, bar charts, line charts, maps, delivery performance metrics.

**Result:**  
Delivered a **single integrated dashboard** combining sales and delivery insights, enabling fast analysis of business performance from multiple perspectives.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Structure

- **KPI Overview**  
  Total Sales, Total Orders, Average Order Value (AOV), Average Delivery Time  

- **Sales by Category**  
  Comparison of revenue contribution across product categories  

- **Top Products**  
  Identification of best-performing products (TOP-10) by sales volume  

- **Sales Trend Over Time**  
  Analysis of sales dynamics, growth patterns, and seasonality  

- **Sales by Region**  
  Geographic distribution of orders with marker size reflecting order volume  

- **Delivery Performance**  
  Evaluation of delivery time and logistics efficiency by region
